Physics-gated surrogates
for wall turbulence.

An investigation into how learned turbulent-flow reconstructions can be evaluated through physical admissibility, wall-resolved error, uncertainty calibration and mechanistic interpretation.

High-dimensional velocity fields make global predictive error an incomplete description of model behaviour. This project brings modelling and evaluation into a common experimental framework: DNS-derived cubes, a shared three-dimensional U-Net backbone, six neural variants and a hierarchy of physical and statistical diagnostics. Wall distance organises the analysis into four regimes, rather than treating the channel as a homogeneous volume.

The research software spans data acquisition and partitioning, GPU training, stochastic inference, risk calibration, spectral and tensor diagnostics, model attribution, robustness analysis and report generation. Two experimental scales serve different purposes: 64³ smoke tests exercise the pipeline; the 96³ primary study uses a separate training, validation, test and calibration allocation. The main channel implementation is a velocity-reconstruction task.

The evidence below connects these modelling choices to the original experimental record. Directly checked histories and arrays are distinguished from manuscript-reported tables and method diagrams, so that each comparison can be traced to the experiment it describes.

CONTRIBUTION & RESPONSIBILITY / ORIGINAL MSc PROJECT
  1. Experimental system. Construct and organise acquisition, preprocessing, modelling, HPC workflows, prediction and reporting across the HIT-to-channel development.
  2. Comparative modelling. Implement a deterministic reference and stochastic/physics-penalised variants within a shared 3D reconstruction setting.
  3. Evaluation framework. Combine wall-band stratification, uncertainty and physical diagnostics; investigate attribution and Germano-style coefficient recovery.
  4. Research analysis. Interpret convergence, uncertainty and physical compliance, and distinguish empirical results from the scope of the proposed framework.

THE RESEARCH ARGUMENT / FOUR CONNECTED QUESTIONS

What does it mean
to trust a reconstructed flow?

This study asks whether a small reconstruction error is enough. The experiment changes how a three-dimensional network learns and expresses uncertainty, then asks whether its fields remain spatially credible, physically admissible and statistically reliable. Each question needs different evidence.

MATHEMATICAL SETTING / MANUSCRIPT METHODS

A physical domain.
A statistical contract.

The underlying channel flow satisfies incompressible momentum balance with no-slip walls and periodic homogeneous directions. The learned reconstruction is tested against selected consequences of that physical setting; it is not a new Navier–Stokes solver.

    Diagnostic definitions, numerical conventions and provenance +

    Velocity gradient and vortical structure

    Let Gab = ∂ũa/∂ξb, S = ½(G + Gᵀ), Ω = ½(G − Gᵀ). The viewer computes ω = ∇ξ × ũ and Q = ½(Ω:Ω − S:S). Gradients use unit array spacing, central interior differences and one-sided boundary differences on the full saved lattice. These are diagnostics of component-standardised arrays, not dimensional flow invariants.

    The original q_criterion.py includes an additional factor of two in its symmetric-tensor contraction. This viewer explicitly uses the conventional Frobenius contraction above. It does not silently present a revised diagnostic as an archived output.

    Geometry, spectra and uncertainty

    Marching tetrahedra linearly interpolates the stride-two display lattice. The percentile control selects an isovalue; it is not a calibrated probability. Reference and mean use the same reference-derived threshold, so switching between them preserves the level. Error and spread use their own quantiles. Reference and prediction share a colour scale. Absolute error and σ use their own nonnegative scales. σ is available for velocity components, not propagated to nonlinear diagnostics.

    Spectral curves remove each component’s spatial mean, apply a 3D FFT and sum ½Σ|û|²/N⁶ in nearest-integer radial mode shells. Parseval holds over all shells; the chart shows modes 1–32. This patch-periodic index-space diagnostic is not a measured channel inertial range. Source hashes and exact transformations ↗

    Read the evaluation and calibration notes +

    Keep evaluation coordinates consistent.

    A neighbouring ensemble path reports RMSE 0.31458 rather than 0.13816. The inspected prediction route requests denormalised targets while leaving predictions in model coordinates. This is a plausible explanation, not a confirmed account of the historical run. The exhibit uses only the internally consistent, checked ensemble_results arrays.

    Separate the manuscript result from the smoke interval.

    The dissertation reports ensemble PICP of 0.91 at nominal 90% and ECE of 0.045. Those are thesis-reported results with a different stated calibration protocol. They are not the same quantity as the uncalibrated ±kσ coverage measured here. The saved smoke conformal script also differs from the manuscript specification: it fits an unscaled cube-average residual, then permits multiplication by voxel-wise σ. It does not establish a matched finite-sample voxel-coverage guarantee.

    RESEARCH JUDGEMENT / OPEN QUESTIONS

    A result needs
    its boundary.

    The strongest account of this work includes what the experiments leave open.

    Reconstruction and dynamics
    The inspected channel loader supplies the same normalised velocity tensor as input and target. Reconstruction quality does not establish future-state prediction, arbitrary boundary-condition response or a replacement for DNS.
    Reynolds-number transfer
    Reτ = 5200 appears in the evaluation design and repository configurations. The manuscript’s conclusion identifies Reynolds-number generalisation as future work; a completed transfer benchmark is not inferred from the title or configuration alone.
    Physical and statistical validity
    Physics penalties encourage compliance. Gate outcomes depend on coordinates, operators and thresholds. Conformal coverage depends on the score/set construction and assumptions; it does not follow automatically from an uncertainty map or a method name.
    Run-level traceability
    Primary histories, smoke fields and manuscript tables represent different evidence. Reconciling their revisions, normalisation, sampling units and benchmark conditions is necessary for exact reproduction.
